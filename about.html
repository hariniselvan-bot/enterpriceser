Our Story

From a Spreadsheets Rebellion to a Global Platform

Stackly began when three operations leads at a multinational manufacturer discovered they were each maintaining the same customer data in different tools — and none of it matched.

What started as an internal dashboard became a mission: one platform where every team sees the same truth, acts on it instantly, and spends their energy on work that matters.

Today, Stackly powers 500+ businesses across 40 countries, processing over a billion workflow events a year — with the same obsession for craft we had on day one.

Our Mission

To give every organization — from scrappy startup to global enterprise — the operational clarity of a world-class company, without the enterprise-software baggage.

Our Vision

A world where technology disappears into the background — where leaders decide in seconds, teams move in harmony, and software feels less like a tool and more like a colleague.
